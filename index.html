On the flight markers: the dashed ✈ lines are scheduled TIR arrival times, not live wheels-down times, so a delayed flight will sit apart from where the tokens actually move. The real "first flight landed" signal is the first drop in the token curve (200 → 198).

On gaps and ranges: capture runs from a home Mac, not a server — on nights or mornings it's asleep, a reading can be hours old before the next one lands. Wherever a value was confirmed only after a capture gap longer than 15 minutes, this dashboard shows it as a range (last known → first confirmed) or a dashed/faded mark, rather than presenting a guess as a precise timestamp. A solid line or a single time means the reading is from continuous 2-minute sampling; a range or "by HH:MM" means the exact moment fell inside an unwatched gap.

Source: TTD public live-status page AirportLiveStatus.aspx. All times IST. scraped_at is our fetch time; ttd_reporting_time is TTD's own published label.
